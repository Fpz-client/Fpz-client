Fpz Client

Your Minecraft, your way.

Fpz Client is an independent launcher project for Minecraft Java Edition on Windows.

The project aims to provide a convenient way to manage game launches and enjoy a customized Minecraft experience.

Microsoft account authentication is intended to let users sign in with their own accounts to access supported Minecraft services.

Fpz Client is an independent third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
